e). Major Finding of Forensic Audit Report dated 22.02.2020 of M/s SLO Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (Advarisk) are as under:
I. Misappropriation of funds and criminal breach of trust :
The Forensic Audit was conducted on a combined basis for the whole of the RL group accounts, namely,
a. RL Jewelers Pvt. Ltd.
b. RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd.
c. RL Gold Pvt. Ltd.
d. RL & Sons &
e. Manraj Jewelers Pvt. Ltd.
M/s Rajmal Lakhichand (RL), a partnership concern, is the flagship firm of the RL group through which majority of sales and purchase transactions of the above RL group entities, were routed, wherein payments were made against purchases, however realization of sale was still outstanding in the accounts of the above RL group entities. (Page 32/33 of the FA report dated 22.02.2020)
As per the Forensic Audit report, during the period FY 2012 to FY 2014, total purchase of the RL group entities from the said partnership concern is Rs. 5,837 Cr while the sales made by the RL group entities to the partnership concern is Rs. 6,696 Cr. The corresponding figures for purchases and sales of R L Jewels Pvt. Ltd. with the partnership firm (M/s Rajmal Lakhichand) being Rs. 834 Cr and Rs. 427 Cr respectively. In the monthly stock & Book Debt statement dated 31.10.2014 submitted by the borrower, M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd., it was confirmed that the entire debtors outstandings were recoverable within 90 days. However, there were negligible receipts and payments in the accounts of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. The old receivables were continuously carried forward by netting against fresh purchases / sales, thereby masking overdue positions. Also, the outstanding sales and purchases were knocked off against the new purchases and sales and the old receivables balance remained outstanding for a long time. This indicates the funds were diverted and thus liability in the loan account remained unchanged.
II. Diversion of funds through current accounts with other banks :
In FY 2013–14, RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. made total sales of INR 73.82 Crores to M/s Precious Hallmarking and Refining Pvt. Ltd. and the sale proceeds were received in the Axis Bank account of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. (A/c No. 174010200010919). During the scrutiny of the Axis Bank account statement for the month of Feb 2014, which was available on the bank records, it has been observed that 23 inward remittances totalling Rs. 67.60 Crores were received from M/s Precious Hallmarking and Refining Pvt. Ltd. On the same date, 27 outward remittances totalling Rs. 66.52 Crores were made to the Partnership Firm, M/s Rajmal Lakhichand. It is evident that the sale proceeds of the borrowing entity were immediately transferred to their partnership firm without depositing them in the loan account of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd.

As per Stock statement dated 30.09.2014 of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd., the receivables with the company were of Rs. 219.14 Crores, inventories of Rs 17.19 Crores and payables of Rs 45.80 Crores. Further, as per Audited Balance Sheet as at 31.03.2015, the receivables with the company were Rs 112.25 Crores, Inventories of Rs 22.96 Crores and payables of Rs 1.04 Crores.
Based on the above, it is evident that RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. was able to recover Rs. 106.88 Crores by reducing their receivables in the intervening period. During the same period, the payables also got reduced by Rs 44.76 Crores. This net excess amount of approximately Rs 55 Crores received by the Company in the above transactions was not routed through SBI loan account. This indicates the funds were diverted and thus liability in the loan account remained unchanged.
III. Alteration and Falsification of books/electronic record :
The company has resorted to irregular accounting practices over the period 01.04.2011 to 31.3.2014, which rendered timely fraud detection difficult. The fraud has been perpetrated though numerous instances of manipulations in the books of accounts of the company over the period. Important events highlighting the modus operandi are enumerated below:
i. As per Stock statement of Feb'2015 and Audited Balance Sheet as on 31.03.2015, the value of receivables of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. were Rs 79.20 Crores and Rs 112.25 Crores respectively, inventories were of Rs 6.55 Crores and Rs 22.96 Crores respectively and payables were Rs 39.50 Crores and Rs 1.04 Crores respectively. The funds, required for increasing receivables and inventories by Rs 49.46 Crores and payment of Rs. 38.46 Crores made to creditors, during the month of March 2015, were not available. Despite the Company reducing their liabilities and increasing their assets during the month of Mar'2015, there were no debits/credits in the loan accounts of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. and the loan outstanding remained unchanged. In their books of account there is no evidence to support the sources of funds.
ii. As per the FA report dt 22.02.2020 Page No. 33, it is observed that M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. had outstanding receivables of Rs. 446 Crores due from its another group entity, M/s Manvi Holdings Pvt. Ltd. and having trade payables to Rs 321 Crores to partnership firm M/s Rajmal Lakhichand as on 31.03.2018 (Page No. 61 & 63 of FR report dated 22.02.2020). Subsequently the inventories were wiped off, however no credits reflected in the Company's loan accounts with SBI. It again indicates the funds were diverted and liability in the loan accounts of M/s RL Jewels Pvt. Ltd. remained unchanged.
